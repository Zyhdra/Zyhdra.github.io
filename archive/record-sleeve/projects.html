Inductiv

Clinical tracking built for nurse anesthesia programs.

Nurse anesthesia students juggle clinical shifts, case logs, preceptor evaluations, and degree requirements, usually in generic tools that weren't built for them and that they can't use quickly between cases. Program directors told me they were paying a lot for that.

So I built Inductiv: one clinical record, from a student's first case to the degree audit. I started with scheduling because students check it every day, then built case logging, evaluations, and degree audits on top of it. Two of the two programs I demoed it to committed to beta testing.

My role
Founder. Product, engineering, and sales, solo
Platforms
Web app and a native iOS & Android app
Stack
TypeScript, Next.js, Hono API, Expo (React Native), Supabase, Railway, Cloudflare
Status
In beta with 2 nurse anesthesia programs in Texas, about 40 students a year

Under the hood

  • API-first monorepo. One backend serves the web and mobile clients, and shared TypeScript types define the data model once for all three.
  • One authorization policy. Every endpoint routes through a single tested permissions function, with no ad-hoc role checks scattered through the code.
  • Program-scoped by design. Each nursing program is its own tenant, and no query returns another program's data.
  • Evaluations as a state machine. Draft → preceptor submitted → admin reviewed → director signed. Signed records are immutable; corrections become addenda.
  • Health data handled carefully. 2FA, encrypted storage, and PHI filtering that checks notes for patient details before they're saved.

Meownvelope

Envelope budgeting for college students. With cats.

Starting to save can feel daunting when every finance app assumes you already know what you're doing. Meownvelope makes the classic cash-envelope method digital: split your money into categories, fill them up, and keep a streak going, with badges and a cat to cheer you on.

We built it as a team of four in Agile/Scrum sprints. I owned page functionality and on-device local storage, wrote unit tests for the storage and gamification subsystems, and demoed features to our faculty advisor each sprint.
