# Surface: personal site (index.html + section pages)

Mode: Persuade. Recruiters skim, decide, and reach out. Person-first: "you, the person" leads.
Owner constraints: brown + blue; simple; smooth & satisfying motion (not gimmicky); avoid corporate/stiff, childish, busy, dark/techy.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Steven as the album. A jazz-LP cover where a blue/brown duotone portrait and a massive cropped name ARE the first viewport; the site's sections are the tracks on the back sleeve. Refuses the default "headshot circle + gradient hero + card grid".

OWN-WORLD: Sleeve blue #1d4e9e and tobacco brown #6b4226 as flat fields owning whole regions; tan #c9a27a for secondary ink; deep ink #0f1b2e; light ground #f4f6f8 (cool, never cream). Heavy condensed sans for display, cropped and off-grid; clean grotesk for text. Duotone photography only. No cards, no boxes: rank by scale and weight. Labels styled as liner-note credits ("Side A · 01").

STORY: Recruiter meets Steven first (portrait, name, one honest line), sees he's an engineer + founder graduating Dec 2026, then flips to the back sleeve: Experience, Projects (startup app, Meownvelope), Personal, and contacts or downloads the resume.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed duotone portrait (blue field, brown shadows), name set huge in condensed caps cropping off the right edge, a small credit block bottom-left (role line, Dec 2026, location), and the primary actions as a short track list: Resume ↓, Email, LinkedIn, GitHub. Nav reads Side A / Side B along the top edge.

FORM: Record Sleeve (1950s–60s jazz LP covers); grounded list position 1 (taken as Impeccable's pick); seed key e27af21a. Signature interaction: the sleeve-slide: changing sections slides the "record" out of the sleeve (a smooth horizontal reveal with the vinyl disc edge peeking out), hover focuses one track and dims the rest.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
