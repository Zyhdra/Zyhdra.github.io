# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: plain static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step. Deploys directly to GitHub Pages and stays easy for the owner to edit by hand.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Steven Kertes for full-time roles starting after his December 2026 graduation. They arrive from a resume, LinkedIn, or application link, usually skimming quickly, often on mobile, deciding whether to reach out.

Target roles: Software Engineering, AI Engineering, AI Solutions / Solutions Engineering, Product/Project Management, Technical Product/Project Management.

## Product Purpose

A personal site that tells recruiters who Steven is: his experience, what he has built, and who he is outside work. Success = a recruiter understands his range (engineering + product + founder) within a minute and reaches out via email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A soon-to-graduate engineer who has also founded a startup with a shipped mobile app: builder and product thinker in one person. That combination, not a generic "developer portfolio", is what the site should make obvious.

## Operating Context

- Linked from resume, LinkedIn, and job applications.
- Hosted on GitHub Pages (static only).
- Recruiters may want a downloadable resume PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Home/About (intro, photo, links to GitHub, LinkedIn, email); Experience (resume as web page + PDF download); Projects (dedicated startup page showcasing the mobile app with a link; Meownvelope write-up); Personal/Fun (grad photos, hobbies).
- Project source code is private (Meownvelope lives in Bitbucket; startup repo is private). Projects are shown via screenshots and write-ups, never code links that 404.
- Open: startup name, link, and app details; Meownvelope details; resume content; photos. Must come from the owner.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner-requested binding direction: brown and blue color palette; simple/minimal but fun to interact with; keep it simple and easy.

## Evidence on Hand

None in the project yet. Resume, photos, app screenshots, and project details must be supplied by the owner. Do not invent employers, metrics, dates, testimonials, or project claims.

## Product Principles

1. Recruiter-first: the essentials (who, what roles, how to contact, resume) are reachable in seconds.
2. Show range honestly: engineer, product thinker, and founder, all backed by real work.
3. Personality earns memorability: the fun parts make him stand out, never at the expense of clarity.
4. Easy to maintain: plain files the owner can update without tooling.
